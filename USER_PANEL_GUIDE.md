# Sabcargo – User Panel Feature Guide

Welcome to the **Sabcargo** User Experience. This document outlines the features and capabilities available to end-users within the mobile application. Designed for speed, security, and ease of use, the Sabcargo User Panel ensures a seamless logistics journey.

---

## 🔐 1. Secure Authentication
Sabcargo prioritizes your data security with a streamlined, passwordless login flow.
*   **One-Time Registration**: Sign up with just your name, email, and phone number.
*   **Email Verification**: Automatic 4-digit code sent to your email to verify identity.
*   **Permanent Access PIN**: A secure 6-digit PIN is generated for your account. This serves as your "forever" key, eliminating the need to remember complex passwords.
*   **PIN Recovery**: Easy reset flow via email verification if you forget your access code.

## 🏠 2. Dynamic Dashboard
The home screen provides an at-a-glance view of your logistics world.
*   **Active Services**: View and select from a range of logistics services (e.g., Sea Freight, Air Freight, Road Transport).
*   **Real-time Announcements**: Stay informed with news and updates from Sabcargo.
*   **Shipment Shortcuts**: Quick access to tracking and new shipment creation.

## 📦 3. Shipment Management & Tracking
Transparency is at the heart of Sabcargo.
*   **Live Tracking**: Enter your tracking number to see the exact status and location of your goods.
*   **Status History**: View a detailed timeline of your shipment from "Booked" to "Delivered."
*   **Shipment Details**: Access comprehensive info including weight, dimensions, and estimated delivery dates.

## 🤖 4. AI-Powered Support (Sabcargo AI)
Need help? Our intelligent chatbot is available 24/7.
*   **Instant Answers**: Ask about shipping rates, restricted items, or office locations.
*   **Natural Language**: Speak to the bot like you would a human agent.
*   **Integrated Logistics Knowledge**: The AI is specifically trained on Sabcargo’s services and logistics best practices.

## 👤 5. Profile & Settings
Customize your experience and manage your personal data.
*   **Personal Info**: Update your contact details effortlessly.
*   **Profile Picture**: Personalize your account with a photo.
*   **Security Settings**: Manage your access PIN and session status.
*   **Clear History**: Option to wipe local tracking history for privacy.

## 📱 6. Modern, Responsive UI
*   **Premium Design**: A clean, vibrant interface with smooth animations (powered by Flutter).
*   **Universal Search**: Quickly find shipments or services from anywhere in the app.
*   **Intuitive Navigation**: Simple bottom navigation for core features: Home, Tracking, Profile, and AI.
